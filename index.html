      About Big Replay
      
  
      Big Replay is a tool for recording character positions in Big Walk and playing them back afterward. It runs alongside the game and pulls data by scraping memory - it's not a mod and doesn't change anything in the running game. To use it, download BigReplay-Setup.exe from GitHub and start the game. Afterward, your replays will be in a BigReplay folder in Documents. Replays you open here are read in your browser and only uploaded if you sign in and choose Share.
